# Direct Precedents and Common-Answer Baseline

## Purpose

Identify the familiar answers used by artillery, physics-puzzle, deterministic
planning, route-optimization, and course-design precedents. This is a baseline
for generating ideas, not a popularity ranking.

## Sources

The primary and first-party sources are linked in the table below.

## Findings

| Family | Primary or first-party source | Supported mechanism | Transfer limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artillery campaign | [Worms W.M.D, Team17](https://www.team17.com/games/worms-w-m-d/) | A thirty-level campaign adds tactical terrain and a large set of weapons, utilities, vehicles, and buildings. | Opponents, movement, and a large arsenal conflict with Paint Mountain's focused single-cannon loop. |
| Artillery training | [Worms W.M.D Mobilize, Team17](https://www.team17.com/news/worms-w-md-mobilize-out-now-on-apple-android) | Separate training and campaign missions combine aim, elevation, power, varied objectives, retry pressure, and three-star goals. | Wind, combat turns, and sudden death are outside the current brief. |
| Artillery-golf | [Team17's Worms Crazy Golf retrospective](https://www.team17.com/news/team17s-100-games-part-thirteen-2011-2012-worms-crazy-golf-worms-for-facebook-and-more) | Authored set-pieces and chain reactions reward discovering efficient routes. The same retrospective reports that character leveling in another Worms project was dropped when it became hard to balance and read. | Golf scoring and scripted objects do not equal continuous terrain coverage. |
| Physics level design | [Rovio, The Craft Behind the Levels](https://www.rovio.com/articles/the-craft-behind-the-levels-a-look-into-level-design-in-rovio-games/) | Designers pace new puzzle pieces, objectives, helpers, obstacles, and available moves, then use playtests and flow data. A clever twist can still create frustration. | Studio practice is not controlled evidence for one ideal curve. |
| Physics construction | [Bridge Constructor Portal, official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/684410/Bridge_Constructor_Portal/) | Sixty chambers progressively layer familiar tools and hazards. | Player-built structures create a much wider action space than a committed launch. |
| Physics sandbox | [Crayon Physics Deluxe](https://www.crayonphysics.com/) | Multiple physically valid solutions and progressively broader tools support experimentation. | Drawn geometry and an editor are outside Paint Mountain's fixed-terrain scope. |
| Deterministic planning | [Into the Breach, Subset Games](https://www.subsetgames.com/itb.html) | Threats are telegraphed so the player analyzes a visible future and learns through retry. | Turn-based grid combat does not transfer as trajectory content; legibility does. |
| Route optimization | [Mini Metro, Dinosaur Polo Club](https://dinopoloclub.com/games/mini-metro/) | Topology and limited resources create difficulty without hiding the state. | Continuous demand differs from discrete shots. |
| Optional expert rules | [Mini Motorways Endless and Expert modes](https://dinopoloclub.com/2022/11/03/play-mini-motorways-your-way-with-our-new-endless-expert-mode-update/) | A separate expert ruleset adds permanence and limitations, while endless play preserves experimentation. | Punitive permanence does not suit the main rapid-retry loop. |
| Golf course strategy | [USGA, Maximizing the Fun Factor](https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/articles/2013/02/course-care-maximizing-fun-factor-at-your-facility-21474854351.html) | Strong holes challenge skilled players while providing a visible bailout or navigable side for less-skilled players. | Real golf execution, repeated strokes, and handicaps do not directly map to a physics puzzle. |
| Golf distance | [R&A, Distance Insights Report](https://www.randa.org/en/articles/distance-insights-report-released) | Challenge should test a broad range of skills; lengthening alone can weaken strategic variety. | This is course-policy evidence, not game-level proof. |
| Orienteering | [IOF Competition Rules 2024, PDF](https://orienteering.sport/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/iof-rules-2024-v1.12-wcup.pdf) | Route choice should reward independent thinking, be assessable from the map, and avoid hidden or ambiguous causes of unpredictable time loss. | Orienteering is navigation under physical execution and imperfect real-world visibility. |

## Common-Answer Baseline

The sampled precedents repeatedly use these levers:

1. Introduce one mechanic, then combine it with known mechanics.
2. Tighten spatial windows through narrower routes, hazards, slopes, or target
   shapes.
3. Add scarcity through shots, moves, time, or limited interventions.
4. Add optional mastery through stars, medals, perfect routes, or expert modes.
5. Keep threats and feedback readable so failure teaches.
6. Tune authored content through blockouts, playtests, and completion data.

## Anti-Patterns and Gaps

- More systems can become difficult to balance and too complex to read.
- A single clever twist can obscure the underlying puzzle and feel arbitrary.
- Opaque post-launch correction would weaken deterministic comparison.
- These precedents rarely combine continuous surface coverage, a committed
  ballistic launch, terrain-driven route changes, and one distant readable 3D
  landform. That gap is the search space for Paint Mountain-specific ideas.

## Limitations

- Most first-party pages describe products and design practice; they do not
  prove that one progression method improves retention or enjoyment.
- Dates and feature lists are contextual evidence. The transferable unit is the
  causal mechanism, not the theme or commercial result.
