# Distant Analogues

## Purpose

Extend the search beyond games. Each item transfers a causal structure only;
it does not justify copying its surface language or institutional process.

## Sources

The studies, standards, and first-party guidance are linked beside each finding.

## Findings

### Prerequisite mastery and bridge tasks

- [Bloom, Learning for Mastery (1968), ERIC record](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED053419)
  frames progression as explicit units, formative attempts, targeted
  correction, and a threshold before the next concept. A later
  [meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543060002265) found positive
  achievement effects but also more instructional time and lower completion in
  some self-paced settings. Transfer: isolate a planning principle, then use a
  bridge stage that adds one new demand. Breakpoint: mandatory repetition would
  conflict with all-open rapid play.
- The [Climbing Wall Association Routesetting Guide](https://www.cwapro.org/blog/shape-the-future-of-routesetting-cwas-routesetting-guide)
  recommends varied examples, bridge climbs, representative forerunning, and
  difficulty that is not only hold size or steepness. Transfer: define a skill
  family, vary route shape within it, then bridge to the next family.
- [Bengio et al., Curriculum Learning (ICML 2009)](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2009/papers/119.pdf)
  supports simple-to-complex example order when early structure transfers.
  [Wu, Dyer, and Neyshabur (ICLR 2021)](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=tW4QEInpni)
  found curricula can produce only marginal benefits and that random or reverse
  orders sometimes compete. Transfer: ordering is useful only when the invariant
  being learned is explicit.

### Estimating a moving skill frontier

- [Levitt's transformed up-down method](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.1912375)
  changes stimulus level after responses to estimate a threshold. Transfer:
  change one controlled demand after success or failure. Breakpoint: fatigue,
  learning, and ambiguous partial coverage violate a stable response model.
- The [International Association for Computerized Adaptive Testing](https://iacat.org/introduction-to-cat/)
  describes ability estimation, calibrated item banks, informative next-item
  selection, and precision-based stopping. Transfer: a future recommendation
  system could distinguish aim, route reading, mechanism routing, and shot
  economy. Breakpoint: the current game has no calibrated stage bank and a miss
  is not a clean right-or-wrong item response.
- [FAA Advanced Qualification Program guidance](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/training/aqp)
  derives proficiency objectives from task analysis, evaluates them in
  scenarios, and revises training from performance data. Transfer: replace one
  global difficulty number with a small competency vector. Breakpoint: this is
  a regulated training system, not evidence about game enjoyment.

### Contrast, transfer, and interleaving

- [Shea and Morgan (1979)](https://doi.org/10.1037/0278-7393.5.2.179) found
  that interleaved motor practice could reduce immediate performance while
  improving later retention and transfer. A later
  [boundary study](https://doi.org/10.1080/14640749208401303) found the benefit
  depends on the structure of variation. Transfer: first establish a stable
  rule, then interleave older and newer route demands.
- [Variation-theory research](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11858-017-0858-4)
  describes contrast, generalization, and fusion: vary one relevant aspect
  while other aspects stay stable, then combine aspects. Transfer: paired
  stages can make the cause of added difficulty visible without tutorial prose.
- [FAA scenario-based training guidance, PDF](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/aviation_instructors_handbook/09_aih_chapter_7.pdf)
  combines realistic planning, execution, assessment, and learner
  responsibility, while retaining focused drills when a fundamental skill is
  missing. Transfer: every later stage can remain a complete planning scenario,
  not an abstract tutorial room.

### Low-cost rehearsal, disturbance, and recovery

- [Seymour et al. (2002)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12368674/) found that
  criterion-based virtual-reality rehearsal improved operating-room performance
  in a small randomized study. Transfer: introduce new route behavior in a
  forgiving stage before combining it with tight constraints. Breakpoint: the
  domain, stakes, and sample are not comparable to a game.
- [Connell's intermediate-disturbance hypothesis](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.199.4335.1302)
  suggests too little disturbance can lead to stagnation while too much can
  destroy the system. Later work found multiple possible curve shapes
  [rather than one universal rule](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3078405/).
  Transfer: alternate consolidation and moderate novelty instead of increasing
  every scalar on every stage.
- [IAEA defence-in-depth guidance](https://www.iaea.org/es/node/281987)
  uses independent layers with distinct objectives and protection against
  common-cause failure. Transfer: added difficulty should preserve separate
  channels for prediction, visible terrain response, coverage feedback, and
  retry. Breakpoint: a game permits failure; too many safeguards can erase
  meaningful challenge.

## Cross-Domain Synthesis

The repeated distant structure is:

`establish one invariant -> vary one relevant aspect -> contrast outcomes ->
transfer to a new surface form -> interleave with prior skills -> combine while
preserving diagnosis and recovery`.

A single scalar does not express mastery of launch calibration, terrain
reading, route choice, mechanism planning, and shot economy. Full adaptive
difficulty would require evidence the project does not have. An authored,
visible, multidimensional sequence is better supported by the current product
and architecture.

## Limitations

- These studies and standards optimize learning, assessment, safety, or
  physical performance, not game enjoyment.
- Their mechanisms are hypotheses for level structure. They still require
  running-game playtests and calibration inside Paint Mountain.
